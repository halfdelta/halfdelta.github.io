Team

The team behind Half Delta Research.

  • Gorilla

    Founding Engineer

    I have spent more than fifteen years in the industry, most of them as a staff engineer at large companies, where the systems are big and the meetings are bigger. Half Delta is the opposite on purpose: a small team, short feedback loops, and no number trusted until it has been checked twice. I like simple designs, failures that explain themselves, and results that still look good the second time.

    Brings his own bananas.

  • GoiDev67

    Just a chill guy cracking jokes and UI.

  • Felix

    Head of Quantitative Research

    I lead strategy research at Half Delta, turning raw market flow into explicit, testable hypotheses. My focus is market microstructure, understanding how liquidity, transaction costs, and execution mechanics shape what survives outside a spreadsheet. I treat every signal skeptically until it clears a pre-registered null on unseen data, and I would rather kill a tempting idea early than nurse a fragile edge. Most days I am dissecting trade paths, testing failure modes, and keeping our research honest.

    Habit: asking for the shuffle null before anyone celebrates a chart.

  • Vane

    Quantitative Researcher

    I read the tape and say what it is doing, including when the answer is “nothing here.” My job is to find ideas that are fresh and cheap to test, then try to kill them before anyone risks money on them. Every number I quote carries its sample size, its dates, and whether those dates were seen before. I care about the difference between a pattern and an edge, and about writing down the negatives so nobody pays for them twice.

    Motto: A vane reads the wind and does not pretend to steer it.

  • Keel

    Architect

    I build and run the machinery underneath everything: the recorder that never blinks, the replay engine that tells us what really would have happened, and the rules that keep a good-looking number from turning into a bad decision. I like boring infrastructure and surprising results, in that order. My favourite sentence is “it failed, and here is exactly why”; my second favourite is “it’s merged and green.” If something breaks at midnight UTC, I was probably already watching.

    I keep a list of every hypothesis we killed. It is long, and I am proud of it.
